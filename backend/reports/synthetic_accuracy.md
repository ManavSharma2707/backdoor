# Synthetic accuracy report

Generated by `python -m seed.accuracy`. **All numbers come from simulated data with a hidden ground truth. They describe how the method behaves on that data. They are not results from real businesses and must never be quoted as customer savings (D-040).**

## Protocol

- Plan date 2026-10-12. The grading window is the last 12 weeks of history, forecast one Monday at a time (7 days ahead) with nothing after the origin visible.
- The method for each segment is chosen on the 12 weeks before the window and event effects are learned from the same earlier weeks, so no choice sees the weeks it is graded on.
- Truth is `synthetic_true_demand`: what customers wanted, including sales lost to empty shelves. The same forecasts are also graded against observed sales on trusted days, the way the app measures itself, to show how censoring changes the picture.
- WAPE is the sum of absolute errors over the sum of demand. Bias is the signed version. Baselines: the 28 day moving average and same weekday last week.

## What this shows

- Aarogya Care Pharmacy: against true demand the selected methods score 76.4% and the 28 day average 76.2%. Against observed sales on trusted days the selected methods score 76.5%, so the app's own error number differs from the truth by 0.2 points.
  On the 5110 product days where an event effect was applied, WAPE is 76.6% with the effects and 76.2% without them.
- Mahalaxmi Super Mart: against true demand the selected methods score 54.0% and the 28 day average 53.9%. Against observed sales on trusted days the selected methods score 53.0%, so the app's own error number differs from the truth by 1.0 points.
  On the 2220 product days where an event effect was applied, WAPE is 49.7% with the effects and 48.2% without them.
- Sanjivani Medical and General: against true demand the selected methods score 95.9% and the 28 day average 95.4%. Against observed sales on trusted days the selected methods score 95.7%, so the app's own error number differs from the truth by 0.2 points.
  On the 3577 product days where an event effect was applied, WAPE is 106.8% with the effects and 106.0% without them.
- Shree Sai Kirana and General Stores: against true demand the selected methods score 66.3% and the 28 day average 65.9%. Against observed sales on trusted days the selected methods score 68.1%, so the app's own error number differs from the truth by 1.8 points.
  On the 2643 product days where an event effect was applied, WAPE is 57.6% with the effects and 56.0% without them.

Reading it: when demand is mostly noise, simple averages are hard to beat, and the parsimony rule keeps the moving average unless another method wins by 3 percent or more on earlier weeks. A method that won on earlier weeks can still lose on later ones. This report says so where it happens and does not tune the choice on the graded weeks. None of this proves anything about real shops. It shows how the method behaves against a known ground truth.
